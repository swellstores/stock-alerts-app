Stock Alerts lets customers ask to be emailed when a sold-out product is back, and tells your team when a product is running low. Customers leave their email address on the product page, and the moment the product or the exact variant they wanted is restocked, each of them gets one email with the product's image, price and a link back to it. Your admins get one email when a product drops to its low-stock threshold, and can opt in to a daily summary of everything still running low.

Everything runs on Swell's own product data and notifications. There's no third-party service, account or API key to connect.

## Back-in-stock emails

When a product is out of stock, a form on the product page saves the customer's email address. As soon as stock comes back, every waiting customer is emailed once, with an intro text you can edit, the product image, the price and a button back to the product.

- **The exact variant.** A customer who asked about a size or colour is emailed when that variant is restocked, not just any variant. You can switch this off so that when the product comes back in stock, everyone subscribed to it is emailed whatever variant they chose. A single variant restocked while the product still has stock elsewhere emails only that variant's subscribers.
- **No duplicates.** Submitting the form twice for the same product doesn't create a second subscription, and nobody is emailed twice for the same request.
- **Already in stock?** If the item is in stock when the form is sent, nothing is saved and your storefront can tell the customer so.

## Low-stock alerts for your team

Set a store-wide low-stock threshold, or give any product its own. When a product's total stock drops from above its threshold to at or below it, your admins get one email with the current stock, the threshold and a link to the product. Further sales don't send more alerts, and the alert resolves itself once stock is back above the threshold.

## A daily digest, if you want one

Switch on the daily digest and your admins get one summary email each morning listing every product that is still running low.

## Everything in your dashboard

- **Products › Stock alerts** lists every subscription, with Waiting, Notified and Cancelled views. Cancel a waiting subscription at any time.
- **Products › Low stock alerts** lists open and resolved alerts.
- Each product gets a **Stock alerts** tab with its own threshold and the customers waiting for it.

## Setup

1. Install the app. Back-in-stock emails and low-stock alerts are on straight away, with a default threshold of 5.
2. Adjust the settings, and set a threshold on any product that needs its own.
3. Add the back-in-stock form to your theme's product page. The README includes a ready-to-paste snippet and the details for headless storefronts.

## Good to know

- Low-stock alerts are per product. For products with variants, the threshold is compared against the product's total stock across all variants, not against any single variant.
- Each subscription is emailed once. Customers can subscribe again after being notified.
- Up to 500 customers are emailed per restock; anyone beyond that is emailed on the next restock.
- Emails are sent through Swell's notifications with your store's sender settings.
- This app is built and supported by Swell and is open source at github.com/swellstores/stock-alerts-app.
